Our Story

Bree Scott, and a little Lyttelton kitchen

Glamour Cake started in Bree's early twenties, back when it was all about wedding cakes. Bree soon noticed something, everyone just wanted a slice. So the wedding tiers made room for cake slices, then cheesecake slices, and then the donuts that Lyttelton now lines up for.

Nearly a decade on, Glamour Cake sits proudly inside the Lyttelton Bakery on Norwich Quay. Every cake, cheesecake and donut is still made by hand, with the same idea that started it all, treats that are made to be different.
