Glamour Cake respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020 (New Zealand).

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that helps us serve you, such as:

  • Your name and contact details when you place an order or get in touch.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to confirm and prepare your order, to communicate with you about it, and to respond to enquiries. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Website and Cookies

This website may use basic analytics and embedded content, such as a Google Maps frame, which may set cookies. These help us understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

4. Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and to protect it from loss, misuse or unauthorised access. We keep it only for as long as needed to provide our services or as required by law.

5. Sharing Your Information

We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate, for example payment providers, only as needed to fulfil your order. We may also disclose information where required by law.
